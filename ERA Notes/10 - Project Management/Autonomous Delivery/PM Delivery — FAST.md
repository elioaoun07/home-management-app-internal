# PM Delivery — FAST

> **V2.1 target plan, revised 2026-09-06.** FAST uses the native-job boundary in [V2 Architecture](<PM Delivery — V2 Architecture.md>) and the protected verification rules in [Evidence & Autonomy](<PM Delivery — Evidence & Autonomy.md>). This replaces the original V2 per-effect execution prescription. It does not alter V1 lanes or permissions.

## 1. Minimum trustworthy loop

**Bind the selected intent → let a native engineer produce a confined candidate → check the requested behavior → reach the authorized destination.** FAST is an effort policy for clear intent, a known implementation pattern and bounded uncertainty. It is not a separate runtime or a promise of low consequence.

A one-line balance change can require stronger evidence than a lengthy read-only investigation. File count, model price and confidence do not determine permission or proof. V2 folds INSTANT's exact-transformation technique into this policy and removes four separate lane infrastructures from the target. The owner normally chooses **Deliver**; mode remains an optional preference.

## 2. Admission without ceremony

Resolve the WorkRef and immutable Contract revision using the selected item and current source fingerprint. The Contract captures outcome, exclusions, scratch/publication scope, criteria and requested disposition. The Grant supplies resource policy, effects and escalation. Use known task templates where they actually fit; do not generate a separate specification and plan merely because V1 had those phases.

A clear item can start under standing policy. A consequential ambiguity requires the missing decision; a routine implementation choice stays with the engineer. The launch cannot silently turn a request for a working control into a text replacement criterion or a deployed fix into a candidate-only promise.

Before dispatch, the supervisor checks executor qualification, confinement, resource admission and the required verification path. A native job's limit must cover the work it can perform internally. Unsupported strict cost guarantees block that promise or require an explicitly authorized alternative; FAST does not make a turn/time cap equivalent to a dollar cap.

## 3. Engineering and protected checks

One native engineering conversation receives the compact launch bundle and can investigate, edit, run its own checks and repair inside the permitted scratch area. Ordinary inner tool calls do not become ERA jobs or individual recovery transactions. Required repository/domain rules remain available; they cannot be discarded to reduce context size.

The candidate cannot publish or change authoritative policy, result storage or verification authority. A final scope check determines what can be accepted for publication; a path prohibited even temporarily must also be denied within the execution boundary. A copied directory or worktree alone is not containment.

The protected checker freezes the returned candidate and executes the appropriate observations. An exact source request may use a qualified transformation witness. A behavior request needs behavioral evidence; an edited filename or zero-test command does not supply it. The writer's own passing logs can guide investigation but cannot mint protected receipts.

Do not require a model reviewer or generated UAT document when qualified checks establish a clear, low-consequence proposition. Add independent semantic challenge when the evidence plan, affected domain or consequence warrants it. If the candidate edits tests, scripts or configuration that influence acceptance, apply Evidence & Autonomy's protection rules so it cannot redefine success.

Initially allow one supervisor-initiated repair after a failed protected check in the FAST pilot, within the Grant and remaining allowance. This is a configurable pilot bound, not a limit of one native edit or one inner test run. A start, resume or repair dispatch has a durable job identity and appropriate reservation. Native ordinary repairs remain inside that job's qualified total bound.

The repaired output is a new frozen candidate. Rerun necessary checks conservatively; defer sophisticated proof-cache dependency tracking until repeated measurements justify it. If a check is unrelated and already failed on the identified baseline, retain that fact, but do not waive a criterion that depends on it.

## 4. Complete journey: quick-amount preset

This is a hypothetical V2 replay of the historically expensive BUD-14 request, not an execution performed by the study.

| Moment | Owner | Native engineer / supervisor | Evidence or failure behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select | Opens “Replace mobile $25 quick amount with $20” and chooses Deliver | Resolves the current item/source and applicable policy | Historical file pointers are hints, not current facts |
| Admit | No repeated explanation | Records displayed amount, intended action value and requested disposition | A working control is the default intent; a source-only request must be explicit |
| Work | Can leave | Native job makes the change in its confined candidate and checks it | No per-edit ERA broker or separate plan/review role is required |
| Check | No action when machine evidence suffices | Protected checker inspects the complete diff and verifies display/action mapping with a qualified witness or mounted/browser interaction | Zero selected tests establish no behavior; screenshot alone establishes appearance |
| Repair | No routine retry approval | A failed check can trigger the bounded, admitted native repair | Preserve failure evidence; do not enlarge scope or modify the acceptance rule to get green |
| Exception | Resolves a real product choice if necessary | A generated/shared preset may expose an affected surface beyond the request | Routine dependency investigation continues; changed intent or prohibited effects require a new decision |
| Result | Sees result and remaining action | Records the exact candidate, named checks and observed disposition | Full transaction persistence is required only if requested or implicated; do not add unrelated proof obligations |
| Destination | Applies/releases if current policy requires it | Supplies the candidate, base identity and prepared handoff | Related source drift requires a successor candidate and rechecks. The product item remains open if its requested disposition is unmet |

Under today's no-Git-write/worktree and owner-release policy, the first useful endpoint is a checked candidate for owner application. A later qualified application path may complete eligible work under standing authorization. FAST itself grants neither integration nor deployment.

## 5. Effort escalation and recovery

Escalate to DEEP DIVE when evidence contradicts the assumed cause, a new domain boundary changes the risk, reproduction exceeds the probe allowance, the change needs an architectural choice or repeated repairs indicate guessing. Preserve the WorkRef, existing decisions, candidate and valid evidence. Increasing effort need not restart the native conversation or create a different execution architecture.

Before every additional native dispatch, recheck current Grant, resource allowance and applicable Contract revision. Broader permissions, spending beyond the Grant or a material product choice require a concrete decision. Investigation within existing authority can continue automatically. Production DB writes remain prohibited in every mode.

Distinguish an engineering problem from an unavailable input. A missing device observation, exhausted resources, unsupported provider control or unknown prior job outcome needs the specific dependency resolved, not a more expensive model. On a lost launch acknowledgment, reconcile the same job; do not issue another paid launch while the first is unknown. Stop withdraws future publication authority, while confirmed executor state determines whether work has stopped.

The [Context & Agent Model](<PM Delivery — Context & Agent Model.md>) defines the lean Checkpoint for a successor. A simple FAST job may need only binding decisions, candidate/evidence references and the next action; native context management stays native.

## 6. FAST build acceptance

The bounded stages live in [Execution Portfolio](<PM Delivery — Execution Portfolio.md>). The FAST path must demonstrate:

- A real clear task reaches a verified candidate with one launch and no intermediate owner decision when standing policy covers it.
- A behavioral criterion cannot pass on filename presence, unrelated green checks or zero tests.
- An extra unauthorized publication change is rejected even if the requested edit is also present.
- A protected-check failure preserves evidence and uses only the allowed repair/resources.
- A stale candidate or unknown dispatch produces a useful recoverable result without duplicate paid work or a false completion.
- The reported result preserves requested versus observed disposition and labels cost/usage according to its actual provenance.

Compare total owner minutes, elapsed time, usable evidence, defects and resources against equivalent native-only delivery. Include environment preparation and review/application effort. No paid warm-up, mandatory phase traversal or second conversation is justified merely to keep the wrapper busy. If confinement preparation dominates small tasks, first measure reuse of an established runner with verified reset; never weaken containment to meet a latency target.
