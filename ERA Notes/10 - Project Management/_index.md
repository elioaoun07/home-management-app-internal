## Rules in one minute

1. One outcome is one row with one lifetime ID, in exactly one campaign checklist. Checkboxes live only in checklists and the Inbox.
2. The Master Book holds what a row means: acceptance, dependencies, evidence, decisions. Plans and research hold no tasks.
3. Code written, migration applied and device behavior observed are separate facts. Only owner-supplied evidence establishes the production database; agents never write to it.
4. Rows only you can close sit under **Needs you**. Built work that waits for your acceptance reads `UAT: pending` in its section.
5. Finished means verified. Then `pnpm pm:ship <ID>`: the row leaves the checklist, the Shipped Log gets its dated line, the criteria move to the archive, and this page refreshes.
6. After editing PM files by hand: `pnpm pm:lint`, `pnpm pm:check-docs`, `pnpm pm:index`.

Full grammar and lifecycle: [_Conventions](<_Conventions.md>). `.pm/` holds runtime files and `_dashboard.html` is generated; neither is a place to edit backlog state.
