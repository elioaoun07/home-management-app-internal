## Reading and disposition

**RECONCILED/LANDED means the study and current PM queue describe the evidence correctly; it does not mean the product defect was fixed.** OWNER/HELD leaves the decision and dependent implementation open. UNVERIFIED names the owner evidence needed. Historical source documents and locked decisions have not been silently rewritten. This register is a cross-reference ledger, not a second backlog; [Coverage](<ASTRA — Coverage & Orphans.md>) records which findings received work.

The load-bearing corrections are that an acknowledgment is not a durable effect, a failed read is not zero, and a stored artifact is not observed completion. A confirm tap, an SDK success response, an accepted Delivery state, and an emitted push all require different evidence. Consolidate their contracts at existing seams; do not add another orchestrator to hide the distinctions.
