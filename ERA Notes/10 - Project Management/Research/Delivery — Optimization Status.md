The engineering is substantially better than the documents that describe it, and substantially less proven than the checkmarks imply. Two of the three headline optimizations — guide-to-builder delivery and full profile propagation — are **genuinely implemented and supersede the gaps their own research documents still report as open**; the third, the prepared-plan route that actually removes an AI job, is complete, well-guarded and **has never run once**. But this is **all uncommitted**, the installed worker image is a week older than the code that requires it, and **not one token of saving has been measured**. Treat the entire slice as reviewed-and-ready, not as in effect.
